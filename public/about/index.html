About

Hi, I’m Tom! (he/him) I’m a Digital Forensics and Incident Response (a.k.a. DFIR) engineer pinging from Zurich, Switzerland. I’ve been working in the cyberz for over a decade.

Most of my work is tied to digital forensics investigations and designing platforms that help with incident response, digital forensics, threat intelligence, and malware analysis. I’m an open-source enthusiast and I contribute to several projects, both professionally and on my free time.

I enjoy solving mysteries, answering questions, building systems that talk to each other, and trying to understand my adversaries.
